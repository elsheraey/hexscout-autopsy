HEXscout prototype

A clickable prototype of every screen the real HEXscout app shipped (60+ routes), rebuilt from scratch with its actual fonts, palette, layouts, and recovered image assets. Every number is fabricated.

Source-recovery artifacts of the real (retired) app are at hexscout/snapshot/ (the bundled web build inside the APK) and hexscout/dump/ (the leak-hunt report + historical JS chunks).

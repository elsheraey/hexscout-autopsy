Privacy

Short version: we don't collect anything we don't need.

What we collect

Anonymous usage metrics via Amplitude (page views, button clicks). No wallet addresses, no portfolio values, nothing tied to your identity.

What we store

Your settings (display currency, default network, notification prefs) are saved locally on your device via Capacitor Preferences. They never leave.

Third parties

Wallet connections use WalletConnect — their privacy policy applies. Price data comes from CoinGecko Pro and PulseX subgraphs.

Trust, audits, longevity

HEX was audited three times: COFI, CertiK and CoinFabrik. All passed.

Audits

Audits don't prove the contract is bug-free — they prove that competent eyes have read the code. HEX's contract is immutable, so a bug found today couldn't be fixed.

Track record

Five-plus years of mainnet operation with no critical exploits is meaningful evidence.

Community

HEX has the trust of a deeply opinionated community. Most public criticism focuses on Richard Heart personally, not the contract itself.

Trading on PulseX

Slippage, MEV, and route selection in practice.

Slippage

Slippage is how much the price can move between when you confirm and when the trade lands on-chain. 0.5% is a sane default for HEX. Big trades may need 1%+.

MEV

Sandwich attacks front-run your trade. Piteas and PulseX V2 protect you. Direct V1 router calls do not.

Route selection

Tools like Piteas check 5+ DEXes and pick the best route. Direct PulseX is fast but rarely the cheapest.

How HEX staking works

When you call stakeStart, your HEX is burned and the contract issues you T-shares. Each day, all active T-shares earn a share of new HEX inflation.

T-share rate

Today's T-share rate is ~5,232 HEX per T-share. The rate increases only — it never goes down.

Daily payout

Every midnight UTC the contract computes inflation (~0.043% of locked supply per day) and pays it pro-rata to all active T-shares.

Penalty mechanics

End within the first 50% of your term → lose more than your principal. End at maturity → no penalty. End 14 days past maturity → ~1%/week.

Community marketing & memes

Why HEX-adjacent communities run their own outreach.

Grassroots

There's no company spending an ad budget on HEX — community members fund billboards, podcast spots and YouTube placements themselves.

Memes

Memes carry the message because the contract itself is, technically, boring (it's just a timer that mints rewards). Pulse animals, the staking ladder image, RH himself — all memes.

Drama

Drama spikes engagement. The community has its share — get used to filtering signal from noise.

Why ladder your stakes

A 'ladder' is a single investment split across multiple staggered stake lengths.

Liquidity

If you stake 1M HEX for 10 years, your money is locked for 10 years. If you ladder 5 × 200K stakes (2y/4y/6y/8y/10y), you get partial liquidity every two years.

Compounding upside

Re-stake matured rungs at the (higher) future T-share rate to compound.

Worked example

1M HEX laddered 1y–8y at today's rates ≈ 27 T-shares total vs 22 T-shares if all 1M went into a single 5y stake.

Self-custody & cold storage

Hardware wallets and how not to lose them.

Hot vs cold

Hot = always-online wallet (MetaMask). Cold = an offline device (Ledger, Trezor) that signs transactions without exposing your private key.

Sticking with hot

Fine for small balances + active staking. Use a long passphrase and store the seed phrase on metal not paper.

Going cold

Recommended above ~$10K. Plug the hardware wallet into a fresh device, copy the seed to two metal plates, store them in two physically separate locations.

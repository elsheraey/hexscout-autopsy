Crypto, for the first time

If this is your first crypto purchase, here's the absolute minimum you need to know.

Wallets

A wallet is just two long numbers — a public address you share, and a private key you guard. MetaMask is a browser extension that manages these for you.

Gas fees

Every blockchain transaction costs a small fee paid to the network's validators. PulseChain fees are typically under a tenth of a cent.

Seed phrase

Your wallet's master backup is a list of 12 or 24 random words. Write it down. Never type it into a website or share it with anyone — not even support.

What is HEX?

HEX is a smart-contract-based certificate of deposit launched on Ethereum in December 2019. Forked to PulseChain in 2023.

Time-locked CDs

You lock HEX for 1–5,555 days and receive T-shares. The contract pays daily yield to active T-shares — there is no validator and no middleman.

Why people stake

Long stakes earn far more T-shares per HEX than short ones (LongerPaysBetter), and large stakes earn slightly more per HEX than small ones (BiggerPaysBetter).

Risks

Endstaking late triggers a small penalty. Endstaking early triggers a large penalty. The price of HEX itself is volatile.

Tax considerations for HEX

This isn't tax advice — it's a map of the events your accountant will care about. Rules vary wildly by country.

Stake-end

When you endstake, in most jurisdictions, the HEX dividend portion is income at fair market value. The principal returning isn't taxable; the dividend is.

Capital gains

Selling HEX for fiat or for another crypto is usually a disposal event.

Lost-record nightmare

If you stake, end, restake, transfer, you'll generate dozens of taxable events. Use a portfolio-tracker like CoinTracker or Koinly from day one.

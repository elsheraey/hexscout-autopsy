Staking

When you stake, your HEX is burned and the contract gives you T-shares. Each day at midnight UTC, the contract pays new HEX rewards to all active T-shares pro-rata.

Two bonuses
BiggerPaysBetter
Larger stake → more T-shares per HEX
up to 10%
LongerPaysBetter
Longer stake → more T-shares per HEX
up to 200%
⏰
End too early → big penalty. End too late → smaller penalty. Plan your stake length.

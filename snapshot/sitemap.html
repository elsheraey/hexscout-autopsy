HEXscout · static snapshot

Every screen of the now-retired HEXscout app (v1.0.34), reconstructed from the Android APK. 62 pages. Click any card to open the live HTML.

Heads up. This is a static snapshot. Pages render their UI shell, but live data calls (CoinGecko, pulsefusion subgraphs, HEXDailyStats, WalletConnect) will fail since the upstream services aren't being called in your browser context. Layout, copy, fonts, icons, and routing all work.
